# M7: hook cleanup (ADR-0055 stage 6c, moved before the features), PLAN (written 2026-11-21)

After M6, every agent of the factory runs either in a task container (build zone) or without a command-running tool
(review zone). The hooks that bound an agent's commands by matching their text guard nothing that the zones do not
already guard. Item: #0-296.

**Before:** M6's exit.

## Parts, in order

### M7.1 The quality hooks' counterparts in CI, recorded
- **What they guard, corrected (review round 3 of #0-299's plan):**
  - `guard-migrations.sh` refuses an edit of an applied migration; its CI counterpart exists and is required:
    `Factory guards` rule 4 (`check-factory-guards.sh`), which the hook's own comment names;
  - `guard-tests.sh` refuses a command that skips verification or runs Maven code a POM did not declare (`exec:`,
    `antrun:`, another `settings.xml`), not a weakened test; on the dispatcher's path the gate is the dispatcher's
    own run, and CI on a fresh runner is the gate that counts, so what an agent skips in its session passes
    nothing. A deleted or weakened test is `Factory guards` rules 1 and 2.
- **What is left:** a seeded case per rule, recorded, that `Factory guards` holds a pull request of the dispatcher's;
  then the hooks go with M7.2. No new CI job is needed.
- **Where:** incident-platform `.github/` (owner), `factory` (the checks' core, for every product, at stage T).

### M7.2 Boundary hooks removed from the factory's sessions
- **What:** remove `readonly-bash.sh`, `guard-protected-bash.sh`, `write-scope.sh`, `read-scope.sh`,
  `guard-write-in-repo.sh`, `guard-git-push.sh`, `guard-factory-scripts.sh` and `isolation-probe.sh` (where M2.1
  replaced it), with their entries and `dontAsk` allow rules in the autopilot settings. ADR-0054 and ADR-0050 are
  amended: the agents' command lists and the closed allow list end.

  What the owner's own sessions on the Mac keep is the owner's choice (`.claude/settings.json`'s denies of the
  owner's commands stay; ADR-0034).
- **Where:** incident-platform `.claude/` (owner), `factory`.

### M7.3 What `check-protected-paths.sh` checks
- **What:** the three copies of the protected list reduce to CODEOWNERS and the dispatcher's own check (#0-278),
  since the deny rules and the hook are gone. `protected-paths.md` says so.
